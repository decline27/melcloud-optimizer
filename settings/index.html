Comfort

Choose the temperature you want and how far it may drift. The app heats more in cheap hours and pauses in expensive ones, always inside that range. The middle is also the "normal heat pump" that savings are measured against.

More variation saves more; less keeps the room steadier.

Electricity Prices

Without a Tibber token the app uses ENTSO-E day-ahead prices (no account needed). Enter your Tibber token to use Tibber's prices instead.

Only needed with more than one home in Tibber. Click "Refresh Homes" after entering your token.

Your network fee per kWh, as on your bill. Savings count it for every kWh the heat pump did not need to buy. With ENTSO-E prices it replaces the typical grid fee in the consumer price.

Hours in the cheapest share of the coming day count as cheap (preheating allowed); the same share of the most expensive hours count as expensive (heating pauses while the room is above your comfort minimum).
